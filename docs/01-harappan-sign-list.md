# 01-harappan-sign-list

## Harappan Sign List Documentation

### Overview

The Harappan script consists of approximately 417 signs in the Mahadevan corpus, with additional variants documented by Wells (694 signs).

### Sign Categories

- **Basic signs**: Simple, standalone symbols
- **Compound signs**: Combinations of basic signs
- **Numeric signs**: Signs representing quantities
- **Pictographic signs**: Signs with clear pictorial meanings

### Sources

- Mahadevan, I. (1977). "The Indus Script: Texts, Concordance and Tables"
- Wells, B. (1999). "An Introduction to Indus Writing"

### Data Templates

The sign templates are located in:
- `data/harappan_signs/harappan_417_signs_template.csv`
- `data/harappan_signs/harappan_417_signs_template.json`
- `data/harappan_signs/wells_694_signs_template.csv`
